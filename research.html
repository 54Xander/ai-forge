Research & Development Directions

Our team combines scientific research with practical engineering and software development. We work across AI-driven scientific discovery, intelligent systems, web technologies and research software, with the goal of transforming research ideas into practical tools, platforms and applications.

01

AI-Driven Drug Discovery

Deep learning, molecular modeling and virtual screening for RNA-targeted small molecules, covalent inhibitors and other AI-assisted drug discovery applications.

02

Web & Platform Development

Design and development of academic websites, research platforms and interactive web applications, with emphasis on usability, maintainability and effective information presentation.

03

AI Agents & Intelligent Workflows

Development of AI agents and automated workflows for research assistance, information processing, knowledge retrieval, task automation and domain-specific applications.

04

Research Software & Applications

Development of research software, scientific utilities, data-processing tools and application-oriented systems that improve efficiency, reproducibility and accessibility.

05

Multimodal AI & Data Intelligence

Multimodal learning, biological sequence modeling and feature fusion for representation learning, interaction prediction and data-driven scientific analysis.

06

Computational & Experimental Integration

Integration of computational prediction with experimental validation, including molecular modeling, single-molecule studies and biological mechanism investigation.
